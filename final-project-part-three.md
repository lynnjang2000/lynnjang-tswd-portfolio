# Changes made since Part II
<p>
  I made several key revisions to strengthen the focus of my project and align it more closely with the needs of the intended audience. 
  First, I changed the targeted audience from talent agencies to record labels, as record labels are directly involved in artist marketing. 
  I believed that an analysis of the growth and strengths of female artists within popular music would be more relevant and actionable for them.
</p>

<p>
  Second, I added my personal experience and interest in the topic. Using my Apple Music Replay data, I explained how I approached the project 
  and what questions guided my analysis. This helped create a clearer connection between my personal listening patterns and the broader 
  industry trends I was examining.
</p>

<p>
  Third, I removed the 2018 example that highlighted the popularity of the hip-hop genre—a year when female artist activity was relatively low—
  and replaced it with 2024 data on the most streamed albums on Spotify. Since many of the top-performing albums in 2024 were by female artists, 
  this new data better supports my argument that female artists represent a high-efficiency segment in the current music landscape.
</p>

<p>
  Finally, in the conclusion section, I added concrete recommendations for record labels. I proposed specific marketing and promotional 
  strategies that could help elevate female artists and better leverage their growing influence in popular music.
</p>

## Final design decisions
<p>
  I kept the same color palette from Part II, but I revised the visual consistency based on the feedback I received. Previously, different graphs used slightly different colors to represent male and female artists—for example, dark green or gray for male artists. For Part III, I standardized the color scheme so that female artists are consistently shown in light green and male artists in gray across all visualizations. This allows viewers to recognize each group immediately without having to refer to the legend every time.
</p>

<p>
  In addition, I incorporated music-related video clips on both the title slide and the conclusion slide. I downloaded these clips from Pexels, and they help set the tone of the presentation while creating a more engaging and cohesive storytelling experience.
</p>
